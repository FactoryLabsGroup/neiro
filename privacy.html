Privacy

Your training stays yours.

Neiro has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and the few Apple services it uses only when you choose to.

Last updated 22 November 2026 · For the Neiro app for iPhone and this website.

  • No accountNothing to sign up for. You open the app and play.
  • No trackingNo ads, no analytics, no third party code. Nothing follows you around.
  • On your iPhoneYour progress is stored on your phone, with a backup in your own iCloud.

Who we are

Neiro is made by Factory Labs. In this policy, “we” means Factory Labs. If you have a question about privacy, write to us at contact@factorylabs.app.

The short version

We don't collect, receive, store or sell any personal data from the Neiro app. There is no Neiro account, and no server of ours that the app reports to. The app is built only from Apple's own frameworks, with no advertising, analytics or crash reporting code from anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Neiro keeps these on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: the games you've played, your scores and best scores, the difficulty you've reached in each game, your skill scores, your level and experience, your streak and streak freezes, the days you trained, your badges, and your daily puzzle and challenge results.
  • Your settings: language, appearance, the skills you chose to focus on, your reminder time, and whether sound and haptics are on.
  • Your duels: the last thirty duels played on this phone, and the name you chose to send with them, if any.

Neiro's widgets read a short summary of this, and a picture of your neuron, from a container on the same phone that only Neiro and its widgets can use. Deleting the app deletes all of it from your phone.

iCloud backup

If you're signed in to iCloud, Neiro copies your progress, compressed, to your own iCloud key value storage, so a new phone or a reinstall can pick up where you left off. The copy sits in your iCloud account, kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We can't see it.

You can turn the backup off in Neiro's Settings, which removes the copy. Resetting your progress in Neiro clears it too.

Game Center and the day's board

The daily puzzle has a board where everyone's first solve of the day is ranked, kept by Apple's Game Center. Neiro doesn't involve Game Center until you open the puzzle, and offers to sign you in only if you're not signed in already and only when you ask.

If you join, Neiro sends Game Center one number for your first solve each day, made from the moves it took and the time. Game Center shows it to other players with your Game Center name. If you allow it, Neiro also shows how your Game Center friends did. iOS asks for your permission first, and you can change it in the Settings app.

Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of it in the Settings app.

Duels

A duel travels in a link that you send yourself, through any app you choose. The link holds the game, the difficulty, a number that deals the same rounds to both players, the language, each player's score and answer counts, and the names you add, if any. Anyone who has the link can read what's in it. Nothing about a duel is sent to us.

Reminders

Daily reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone itself. No server sends them. You can change their time or turn them off in Neiro, or turn off notifications in the Settings app.

Siri and Shortcuts

“Start today's Neiro training” works through Apple's App Intents on your phone. Neiro only receives the request to open today's training. What you say to Siri is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in the Settings app, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics about Neiro with us, without identifying you, as Apple describes. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

What Neiro doesn't do

  • Ask you to create an account or sign in, except to Game Center if you choose to join the day's board
  • Show advertising
  • Use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers
  • Track you across other apps or websites
  • Sell or share your data
  • Ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone

Children

Neiro doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself. If you pick a language, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage, on your device. The games on the page run in your browser and send nothing anywhere. Like any website, the service that hosts it may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, to keep it running and secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
